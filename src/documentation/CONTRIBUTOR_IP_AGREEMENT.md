## 1. Purpose

The Contributor wishes to contribute software, documentation, game content, artwork, audio, designs, ideas expressed in protectable form, or other materials to the Shapeships project (**Shapeships**).

The Parties want ownership of Contributions to be clear so that Shapeships can be developed, modified, distributed, licensed, commercialised, transferred, or otherwise used by the Owner without fragmented intellectual property ownership.

In consideration of the Contributor being permitted to participate in and contribute to Shapeships, and the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

## 2. Contributions

**Contribution** means any material created or supplied by the Contributor for inclusion in, use by, or development of Shapeships during the collaboration, including:

- source code, scripts, tests, configuration, tools, and software;
- documentation and technical material;
- game rules, text, lore, dialogue, names, data, and other authored content;
- graphics, illustrations, icons, interface assets, animations, models, and designs;
- audio, music, sound effects, and recordings;
- specifications, prototypes, mock-ups, and other project materials; and
- modifications, improvements, adaptations, or derivative material relating to Shapeships.

A Contribution includes material submitted through commits, pull requests, patches, messages, file transfers, shared documents, or any other method used for the collaboration.

This Agreement applies to Contributions created on or after the Effective Date.

Any earlier Contributions intended to be covered by this Agreement should be identified in **Schedule 1**.

## 3. Assignment of Intellectual Property

The Contributor assigns to the Owner, absolutely and for the full period of the relevant rights, all right, title, and interest that the Contributor has or acquires in each Contribution, including all copyright and all other intellectual property rights capable of assignment.

To the extent a Contribution gives rise to future copyright or another future assignable intellectual property right, the Contributor assigns that future right to the Owner immediately upon the right coming into existence.

The assignment includes the right for the Owner to:

- use, reproduce, modify, adapt, translate, combine, publish, communicate, distribute, display, perform, and otherwise exploit Contributions;
- incorporate Contributions into Shapeships or any other version, edition, platform, product, or service;
- license or sublicense Contributions;
- sell or otherwise commercialise Shapeships and Contributions;
- transfer or assign the rights to another person or entity, including a future company that owns or operates Shapeships; and
- decide whether, when, and how a Contribution is used, changed, released, or removed.

The Contributor retains no ownership interest in the intellectual property assigned under this clause.

## 4. Pre-existing Materials

The assignment in clause 3 does not transfer ownership of material that:

1. was created by the Contributor independently of Shapeships before the Effective Date; and
2. is identified to the Owner in writing before it is incorporated into a Contribution.

Such material is **Pre-existing Material**.

The Contributor must not incorporate Pre-existing Material into Shapeships without first telling the Owner.

If the Owner approves its inclusion, the Contributor grants the Owner a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free licence to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, combine, publish, distribute, commercialise, and otherwise exploit that Pre-existing Material as part of, or in connection with, Shapeships.

The Contributor should identify any approved Pre-existing Material in **Schedule 2**.

## 5. Third-party Material and Open-source Software

The Contributor must not knowingly include third-party material in a Contribution unless:

- the Contributor tells the Owner what the material is and the licence or other terms that apply to it; and
- its inclusion has been approved by the Owner where the licence could materially affect how Shapeships may be used, licensed, kept proprietary, or commercialised.

Without prior written approval, the Contributor must not introduce material under terms that would require Shapeships source code, proprietary assets, or derivative works to be disclosed, licensed, or distributed to third parties.

This clause does not prevent ordinary use of third-party packages or dependencies already approved for the Shapeships project.

## 6. Moral Rights

The Contributor acknowledges that moral rights may remain with the individual creator even where copyright is assigned.

To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor genuinely consents to the following specified acts or omissions by the Owner, the Owner's licensees, successors, assignees, and persons authorised by them in relation to Contributions:

- editing, altering, adapting, translating, arranging, reformatting, cropping, compressing, extending, shortening, or otherwise modifying a Contribution;
- combining a Contribution with other material;
- changing, replacing, or removing parts of a Contribution;
- using a Contribution in a context, format, platform, or product different from that originally contemplated;
- deciding not to publish or use a Contribution;
- publishing or using a Contribution without identifying the Contributor as its author;
- using collective, project-level, pseudonymous, abbreviated, or no attribution;
- changing the manner, location, prominence, or wording of attribution; and
- allowing any of the above acts or omissions to be carried out by another person authorised by the Owner.

This consent applies to Contributions existing when this Agreement is signed and to Contributions of the descriptions in clause 2 that are created during the collaboration after this Agreement is signed.

Nothing in this clause permits false attribution of authorship to another person contrary to applicable law.

## 7. Contributor Credit

The Owner may credit the Contributor for their work and intends to provide reasonable project credit where practical.

Unless the Parties separately agree otherwise in writing, however:

- no particular form, placement, duration, or prominence of credit is guaranteed; and
- credit does not create or preserve an ownership interest in a Contribution.

## 8. Contributor Warranties

The Contributor warrants that, to the best of their knowledge:

- they are entitled to enter into this Agreement;
- each original Contribution supplied by them is their own work or is otherwise lawfully supplied;
- they have not previously assigned rights in a Contribution in a way that conflicts with this Agreement;
- they will disclose third-party or Pre-existing Material as required by this Agreement;
- they will not knowingly contribute material that infringes another person's intellectual property rights; and
- entering into this Agreement does not knowingly breach another agreement binding on them.

The Contributor does not warrant that every Contribution will be error-free or suitable for a particular purpose.

## 9. Confidentiality

The Contributor must keep confidential any non-public Shapeships information obtained through the collaboration, including:

- non-public source code and repository material;
- unreleased assets, features, content, and designs;
- credentials, keys, tokens, infrastructure information, and security information;
- private development discussions and internal planning material; and
- other information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

This obligation does not apply to information that:

- becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement;
- the Contributor can show they lawfully knew before receiving it through Shapeships;
- is independently developed without use of Shapeships confidential information; or
- must be disclosed by law.

If disclosure is legally required, the Contributor should, where lawful and reasonably practical, notify the Owner before disclosure.

The Contributor must not publish or use confidential Shapeships information for another project or purpose without permission.

## 10. Repository and Project Access

Any access given to the Contributor to Shapeships repositories, services, files, systems, or accounts is for authorised project work only.

The Contributor must:

- take reasonable steps to protect access credentials;
- not share private repository or service access with another person without permission;
- not intentionally copy or retain confidential project materials for unrelated use; and
- stop using project access when the Owner withdraws it or the collaboration ends.

Ending repository access does not affect intellectual property rights already assigned under this Agreement.

## 11. No Employment, Partnership, Equity, or Revenue Share

Unless the Parties enter into a separate written agreement, this Agreement does not create:

- employment;
- a partnership or joint venture;
- an agency relationship;
- ownership of Shapeships;
- equity or shares;
- a royalty;
- a revenue share; or
- an entitlement to payment.

Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Parties from separately agreeing to payment or other commercial terms for particular work.

## 12. Other Projects

The Contributor remains free to work on other games, software, and projects.

The Contributor must not use or disclose Shapeships confidential information or Shapeships proprietary material in those other projects unless the Owner gives permission.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers to the Owner intellectual property created independently by the Contributor that is not a Contribution and does not incorporate Shapeships proprietary or confidential material.

## 13. Further Documents

The Contributor agrees to reasonably assist the Owner, at the Owner's expense, by signing documents reasonably required to confirm, record, or give effect to the intellectual property assignment in this Agreement.

This obligation continues after the collaboration ends.

## 14. Ending the Collaboration

Either Party may end the collaboration at any time unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Ending the collaboration does not:

- reverse or terminate an assignment already made under this Agreement;
- revoke a licence already granted to the Owner under clause 4;
- remove the Owner's right to continue using or commercialising Contributions; or
- end clauses that are intended by their nature to continue, including intellectual property, moral-rights consent, confidentiality, and further-assurance obligations.

## 15. Entire Agreement and Changes

This Agreement records the Parties' agreement about ownership and use of Contributions to Shapeships.

It does not replace separate written agreements dealing with matters such as payment, employment, contracting scope, or other commercial terms unless those agreements expressly say so.

Any change to this Agreement must be recorded in writing and agreed by both Parties.

## 16. Severability

If part of this Agreement is invalid or unenforceable, that part is to be read down to the extent reasonably necessary. If it cannot be read down, it may be severed without affecting the remainder of the Agreement, to the extent permitted by law.

## 17. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[State or Territory], Australia**.

The Parties submit to the courts having jurisdiction there.
