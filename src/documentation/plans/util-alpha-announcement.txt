###Alpha is now live!

This has taken months of work. Way more than I thought, actually. Coding with AI is certainly not ideal, but I found my own way to make it work.
Development is continuing, pretty much daily. There is still a lot to do, as you can see under 'Development Roadmap' in the app.

###v1.3 Rules update.
The app has allowed a much deeper exploration of the meta. I've also been teaching AI the meta through Battle Log analysis. So we needed a bunch of balance changes.

- Faster online play means games can go much bigger than they usually do on paper. Some large ships that looked unattainable are very reachable at higher levels of play, so *Leviathan*, *Ark of Destruction*, and *Ark of Domination* have been nerfed. They are all still very strong.
- Xenites have received a few buffs. The 2-cost *Xenite* ship now heals 1 when built.
- Xenites now have the new *Sacrificial Pool*, giving them a way to destroy opponent ships. This was a much-needed alignment between the species.
- Centaur's *Ark of Knowledge 3* proved itself quite a problem (healing and damage are equal to the higher of the two). Had to nerf. It's now a more simplified Reroll extension, closer in scope to *Chronoswarm 3*.
- Various changes to ship timings, *Chronoswarm* changes, and smaller rules cleanups.

I'll share my thoughts on the meta in the next few weeks.

Super keen to hear everyone's thoughts once you've explored. Discord will be the main place for feedback and finding opponents.
